# How to Connect Your Azure Account

Connecting your Azure account to Pegasus takes just a few clicks. You'll be redirected to Microsoft's secure login page to authorize access.

---

## What You'll Need
- An Azure account with an active subscription
- Permission to access resources in your subscription

---

## Steps to Connect

### 1. Open Cloud Settings
Go to **Settings** → **Cloud Infrastructure** in Pegasus.

### 2. Click Connect
Find the **Microsoft Azure** card and click **Connect Account**.

### 3. Sign In with Microsoft
A popup will open with Microsoft's login page:
- Enter your work or personal Microsoft email
- Complete any two-factor authentication if prompted

### 4. Authorize Pegasus
Review the permissions Pegasus is requesting:
- **Manage your Azure resources** — allows Pegasus to create and view resources
- Click **Accept** to continue

### 5. Done!
The popup will close and your Azure account will show as **Connected** in Pegasus.

---

## What Can Pegasus Access?

After connecting, Pegasus can:
- ✅ View your subscriptions and resource groups
- ✅ Create resources like databases, storage, and AI services
- ✅ Monitor resource status and health
- ✅ Delete resources you create through Pegasus

Pegasus **cannot**:
- ❌ Access your personal files or emails
- ❌ Make purchases without your approval
- ❌ Access resources outside the subscription you authorize

---

## Disconnecting Your Account

To remove Pegasus access:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Cloud Infrastructure**
2. Click the **⋮** menu on the Azure card
3. Select **Disconnect**

You can also revoke access directly in Azure:
1. Go to [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com)
2. Search for **Enterprise Applications**
3. Find "Pegasus" and remove it

---

## Troubleshooting

**Popup was blocked?**
- Allow popups for Pegasus in your browser settings
- Try the connection again

**"You need admin approval"?**
- Your organization may require IT approval for new apps
- Contact your Azure administrator

**Connection failed?**
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting
- Make sure you have at least one active Azure subscription
