## 3. Profitability & Sustainability Analysis

### Token Unit Economics (Cost vs. Revenue)
- **Average Cost of Tokens (API)**: ~$0.01 to $0.02 per 1k tokens (depending on model usage).
- **Pro Plan (200k tokens)**:
    - Revenue: $10.00
    - Est. Cost: $2.00 - $4.00
    - **Gross Margin**: **60% - 80%**
- **Pro+ Plan (600k tokens)**:
    - Revenue: $29.00
    - Est. Cost: $6.00 - $12.00
    - **Gross Margin**: **58% - 79%**

### Sustainability Strategy: Heavy Usage Surcharge
The **25% Heavy Usage Surcharge** is triggered when an account manages >1,000,000 tokens (1.5M for Pro+). 
- **Purpose**: Offsets the infrastructure load of maintaining high-cardinality vector embeddings and long-term storage of heavy context.
- **Impact**: Ensures that the top 1% of "whale" users remain profitable even as their data density increases exponentially.

### Storage Margins
- **Storage expansion** ($5/GB/mo) is highly profitable. Cloud storage costs are typically <$0.02/GB/mo. 
- **Margin**: **>95%**. This helps cross-subsidize the compute-heavy AI analysis.
