# Yearly Summaries 2025

## US
US market showed strong growth in the West Coast due to the new influencer campaign.

## UK
UK sales were impacted by Brexit logistics but recovered in Q3.

## Finland
Domestic market is saturated but stable. High demand for Sauna Robes.

## Asia
Rapid growth in Japan and South Korea. Hoodie line is top seller.

## Australia
Summer collection performed well during their winter. Counter-seasonal strategy working.
